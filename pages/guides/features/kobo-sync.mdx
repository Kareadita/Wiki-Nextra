# Kobo Sync <TagBadge major={0} minor={9} patch={2} />

Kavita can be used as sync server for your Kobo, taking over the role of the official Kobo servers.

## Features

- Sync epubs and archives to your Kobo
- Sync all or some libraries. Or sync only series in a collection of choice
- Optional forwarding of request to the Kobo Store if not handled by Kavita
- Precise 2-way progress sync
- Recommendations (Kavita+)

## Setup

<Callout type="warning">
    Kobo Sync may be disabled by an administrator
</Callout>

Connect your Kobo to your computer, and open `.kobo/Kobo/Kobo eReader.conf`. Under `[OneStoreServices]` find or create the following line

```
api_endpoint=https://storeapi.kobo.com
```

Then replace `https://storeapi.kobo.com` with your Kavita url.

```
api_endpoint=https://kavita.example.com/kobo/<AuthKey>
```

Find out how to obtain an auth key [here](../user-settings/3rdpartyclients.mdx#auth-key)

<Callout type="warning">
    It is recommended to only use Kobo Sync over https!
</Callout>

### Kepubify

Epubs are automatically converted to kepubs when downloaded on your Kobo, Kavita ships with the `kepubify` binary to do this. An administrator may provide a custom binary in server settings.

The binary is included in all our releases. (Docker & Native)

## Preferences

### Sync Preferences

To prevent your Kobo from having to load *ALL* your books, you can restrict syncing to specific libraries or to series in a collection of choice.

<Callout type="info">
    Using a collection is recommended as it gives you most freedom and shorter syncs.
</Callout>

### Progress Sync

The progress sync for archives is per page, and will translate perfectly.

Progress sync for epubs is always guaranteed on the chapter, and when kepubify is available it's best-effort for in chapter progress.
When reading on Kavita Kobo progress is synced every 5m.

### Recommendations (Kavita+)

Recommendation will only show up on your Kobo while using the Collection sync. Kavita will send back the first book of all recommendation series not yet in your collection,
if you decide to read a book simply click add/restore on your kobo and the series will be added to the collection and all its book synced to your Kobo.
