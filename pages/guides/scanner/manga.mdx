### Manga Structure
<Callout type="info">
	 Make sure you understand the [folder requirements](/guides/scanner#file-layout) of Kavita
</Callout>


A good structure would be:
```
Library Root
  ┖── Series Name
      ┠── Series Name v01.cbz
      ┠── Series Name v02.cbz
      ┠── Series Name v03.cbz
      ┖── Specials
        ┖── Series Name Omakes SP01.cbz
  ┖── Series Name 2
      ┠── Series Name 2 Vol.01 Ch.1.cbz
      ┠── Series Name 2 Vol.01 Ch.2.cbz
      ⋮
      ┖── Series Name 2 Vol.02 Ch.6.cbz
```

<Callout type="info">
	Besides `v`/`vol`/`volume`, Kavita also recognizes French `t`/`tome`, Japanese `巻`, Chinese `卷`/`册`, Korean `권`/`장`/`시즌` (season), Thai `เล่ม`/`เล่มที่`, and Russian `Том`/`Тома` as volume markers, and their Chapter equivalents (`ch`/`chapter`/`c`, Japanese `話`, Chinese `话`, Korean `화`/`회`, Thai `บทที่`/`ตอนที่`, Russian `Глава`).
</Callout>
